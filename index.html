About

I'm an undergrad in Computer Engineering at Federal University of Paraíba, and my core focus lies in algorithms, data structures, and complex problem-solving.

As an active participant in competitive programming, a major milestone was co-founding my university's competitive programming community in 2023, which I continue to lead today. I have also competed in various national and international contests, resulting in top rankings and medal placements.

More recently, my interests have expanded into Artificial Intelligence. By working with organizations like B3 and Huawei, I am applying my skills to real-world industry challenges.

Projects

B3

Commodity demand forecasting

Artificial Intelligence Intern, remote from São Paulo, Aug 2025 to present

Built and trained a prediction model on purchase history since 2017 to anticipate which commodities each institutional client will request.

Used it to replace the manual monthly allocation with a Python and Pandas pipeline, cutting requests for unallocated commodities by 50% and saving both money and team time.

Random Forest, Scikit-learn, Python, Pandas
